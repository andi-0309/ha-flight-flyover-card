# Aircraft model credits

The card code is licensed under [MIT](LICENSE). **3D aircraft assets are third-party works**, loaded on demand and subject to their own licenses:

- **[AMVLab aircraft-models](https://github.com/amvlab/aircraft-models)** — neutral Airbus A320/A350/A380 and Boeing 737/787 models, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **[Skytrails Aircraft Models](https://github.com/stagworksde/skytrails-aircraft-models)** — lightweight airliner, regional, business jet and general-aviation GLBs derived from FlightGear AI traffic, [GPL-2.0](https://github.com/stagworksde/skytrails-aircraft-models/blob/main/COPYING.md). Models are downloaded from pinned release v0.1.0. Original source, attribution and conversion scripts are in the linked repository.
- **[Flightradar24 3D Models](https://github.com/Flightradar24/fr24-3d-models)** — individual Boeing 747-8, 777, Airbus A330-200, Embraer E170, ATR, CRJ and related aircraft variants, [GPL-2.0](https://github.com/Flightradar24/fr24-3d-models/blob/master/LICENSE). Original sources and credits are included in that repository.
- **[God's Eye View aircraft assets](https://github.com/WorldPixelMap/android-gods-eye-view/blob/main/public/models/README.md)** — optimized Cessna 172 and Bell 206 fallback models, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (original creators and modifications are credited by the source project).

The card does not bundle or modify these source GLB files. At runtime, they may be rotated and scaled for consistent display. When a type-specific model is unavailable or a download fails, a related aircraft family may be shown as an approximation. Third-party model authors are not affiliated with this project.
