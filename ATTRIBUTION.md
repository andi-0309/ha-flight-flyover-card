# Aircraft model credits

The card code is [MIT-licensed](LICENSE). Aircraft GLB assets are third-party works, loaded on demand and covered by their respective licenses:

- [AMVLab aircraft-models](https://github.com/amvlab/aircraft-models) — neutral Airbus A320/A350/A380 and Boeing 787 models; [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- [Flightradar24 fr24-3d-models](https://github.com/Flightradar24/fr24-3d-models) — detailed Boeing 747/777, Airbus A330, Embraer E-Jet, CRJ, turboprop and other variants; [GPL-2.0](https://github.com/Flightradar24/fr24-3d-models/blob/master/LICENSE).
- [FlightAirMap 3D Models](https://github.com/Ysurac/FlightAirMap-3dmodels) — additional aircraft, including Cessna 182/208, Piper PA-18, Cirrus SR22, Pilatus PC-12 and more. Individual aircraft folders provide their licenses and model/source credits; many originate from FlightGear and FGMEMBERS under GPL-2.0.
- [God's Eye View aircraft assets](https://github.com/WorldPixelMap/android-gods-eye-view/blob/main/public/models/README.md) — optimized Cessna 172 / Bell 206 models, including original creator credits and modification notices; [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

GLB files are **not bundled or modified** by this MIT-licensed card. The runtime adjusts their orientation and scale, and may select the closest available airframe when an exact variant is not available. Original creators and model rights remain with the respective projects.
