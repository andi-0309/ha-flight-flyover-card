# Aircraft model credits

The card code is [MIT-licensed](LICENSE). Aircraft GLB assets are third-party works, loaded on demand and covered by their respective licenses:

- [AMVLab aircraft-models](https://github.com/amvlab/aircraft-models) — neutral Airbus A320/A350/A380 and Boeing 787 models; [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- [FlightAirMap 3D Models](https://github.com/Ysurac/FlightAirMap-3dmodels) — additional aircraft and helicopters, including Airbus EC135/H135, Bell 407, Gazelle, Cessna 182/208, Piper PA-18, Cirrus SR22, Pilatus PC-12 and more. Individual aircraft folders provide licenses and source credits; many originate from FlightGear and FGMEMBERS under GPL-2.0.
- [God's Eye View aircraft assets](https://github.com/WorldPixelMap/android-gods-eye-view/blob/main/public/models/README.md) — optimized Cessna 172 / Bell 206 models, including original creator credits and modification notices; [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

GLB files are **not bundled** by this MIT-licensed card. At runtime, the card adjusts their orientation and scale and may select the closest available airframe when an exact variant is not available. The rescue helicopter is a **runtime yellow recoloring of the H135 fuselage**, with rotor and glazing preserved; it is an ADAC-inspired illustration, **not an official ADAC-branded model**. Original creators and model rights remain with the respective projects.
